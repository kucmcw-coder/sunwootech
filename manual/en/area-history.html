Measurement history and capture

There are two ways to keep a result. Use measurement history if you may need to edit it later, and capture for a picture to share.

📌 Measurement history Pro📸 Capture
What is savedThe point coordinatesA picture of the map
Edit laterYes (load it and continue or edit points)No
Where it goesMeasurement history list (up to 200)Phone gallery and the Records tab
Good forRevisits, corrections, re-measuringReports, sharing in chat apps

Saving to history Pro

  1. After measuring, tap “🛠️” → “📌 Measurement history”.
  2. Enter a name you will recognise (owner, field name) and save. Address, time, area and number of points are saved too.

Loading

  1. In the “📌 Measurement history” list, tap Load.
  2. If something is already drawn, the app asks before replacing it. You can still go back with “↺ Undo”.

Above 200 entries the oldest one is deleted automatically.

Capture

“📸 Capture” on the left saves the map without buttons as a picture in your gallery.

The free version keeps up to 5 pictures in the in-app album. Pictures are still saved to your gallery.
