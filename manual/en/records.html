Viewing and sharing records

Screen captures you save in the app are collected in the “Records” tab. The same images are also saved in your phone's gallery.

View and share

  • Tap an image in the list to see it full size.
  • Use the share button to send it by chat app, message or email.
  • Delete records you no longer need. Deleting in the app does not delete the image from your gallery.
  • Use the search box to find records by address, area or date.
Saving to the gallery needs the Photos/Media permission. If saving fails, allow it in phone Settings → Apps → Smart Area Measure → Permissions.
The free version keeps up to 5 images in the in-app album. Above 5, delete some to keep new ones (gallery saving continues).
