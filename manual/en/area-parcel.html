Auto-draw parcel

Draws a parcel's cadastral boundary with one tap instead of tapping its corners. Pro Korea · Brazil

In Korea it uses the national cadastral map (or FarmMap field boundaries); in Brazil it uses CAR rural parcels. In other countries there is no parcel data, so measure by tapping points or walking.

One parcel — long press

  1. Press and hold inside a parcel for 1–2 seconds. Its boundary is drawn as a red zone.
  2. Check the area in the status bar.

Even if other zones are already drawn, a long press adds the parcel as a new zone.

Several parcels — auto-draw button

  1. Tap “✨ Auto parcel” on the left. In Korea a bar [📐 Cadastral | 🌾 FarmMap | ℹ️] appears at the top: cadastral draws the registered parcel, FarmMap draws the actually farmed field read from aerial photos.
  2. While the button is on, tap parcels one after another. Each tap adds one parcel and the areas are added together.
  3. When finished, tap “✨ Auto parcel” again to turn it off.

Parcel info card (ℹ️)

After drawing, a one-line summary appears at the top — tap it (or “Details ▾”) to expand, “▴” to fold. Tap “ℹ️” to open the full card (area, location, parcel code, source photo year, etc.). Use ‹ › to switch zones. “🕘 Past imagery of this parcel” switches the map to older photos of the same place (Korea: aerial photos by year; Brazil: Esri imagery by date).

Brazil — CAR parcels Brazil only

In Brazil the map list shows “🇧🇷 CAR parcels (Brazil)”, which overlays CAR boundaries on satellite imagery. Turn on “✨ Auto parcel” and tap a parcel to draw it; the ℹ️ card shows declared area, municipality/state, status, type, fiscal modules, registration date and code. CAR boundaries are declared by owners and may differ from reality.

Fitting the parcel to the actual field

If only part of the parcel is farmed, or the boundary does not match reality, adjust it with “✂️ Point edit”: tap the boundary line to add a point and drag points to move them. See Point edit.

If you also draw the same parcel by walking, the overlapping part is counted only once.

When it does not draw

  • Long public strips such as roads, ditches, rivers and railways can also be drawn automatically, but they come out much larger than the farmed part. Trim them with “✂️ Point edit” or tap the corners by hand.
  • Without internet the parcel data cannot be loaded.
