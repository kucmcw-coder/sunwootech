Maps and offline mode

Change the background map with the map button to the right of the search box. Your zones stay when you switch maps.

Which map to use

  • Satellite map — best for seeing field edges, greenhouses and tree rows.
  • Satellite images differ in date and sharpness. If a boundary is unclear, try another map.
  • Kakao / Naver maps and cadastral maps Korea only — Korean maps with parcel boundaries. Outside Korea the app uses the Google satellite map.
  • Past aerial photos Pro Korea only — aerial photos by year from 2011, provided by Korea's National Geographic Information Institute.

Searching for a place

Type an address and press the search (Enter) key, then tap a result. You can also type coordinates as latitude, longitude (e.g. -23.5505, -46.6333) to jump straight there.

No internet (offline mode)

When the connection drops in remote areas, the app switches to offline mode automatically. The background map is not shown, but you can keep measuring by walking with GPS and tapping points. The map comes back when the internet returns.

Address search and auto-draw parcel do not work offline.

Compass

The compass on the left shows which way the phone is pointing (magnetic north). The map always has north up. You can hide the compass in “🎛️ Display”.
