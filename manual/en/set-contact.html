Reporting a bug and contacting us

Tell us what is inconvenient, with a screenshot if you can. Many features were fixed thanks to user reports. You can write in English, Spanish or Portuguese.

Report a bug

  1. In the Settings tab, tap “🐛 Report a bug”. A report form opens.
  2. Describe what you were doing, what you expected and what happened.
  3. Attach a screenshot. It helps us find the cause fastest.

Email

“✉️ Email us” opens an email with the app version and device information already filled in. Please keep that information and just add your message.

If you like the app, a short review on the Play Store helps us a lot.
