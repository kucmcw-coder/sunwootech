Units, language and display

Area and length units

In “⚙️ Settings” → “Area / Length Units”, choose the main unit, an optional second unit and the length unit: m², hectare (ha), acre (ac), ft², pyeong / m, km, ft, yd, mi. Changes apply to the map immediately.

Language

The app supports English, Español, Português and 한국어. It starts in your phone's language; change it under “🌐 Language” in “⚙️ Settings”.

Map display

“🎛️ Display” on the left of the measure screen turns these on and off:

  • 💡 Hide labels: text next to the buttons
  • 🏷️ Hide area: area and length labels on the map
  • 📍 Hide location: your location arrow
  • 🧭 Show / hide compass
