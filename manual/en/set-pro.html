Subscription

  • Monthly and yearly plans are available. The yearly plan has a 7-day free trial the first time you subscribe.
  • Payment and cancellation are handled by your Google Play account. “💳 Subscription” in the Settings tab opens the Play Store subscription page.
  • Your subscription follows your Google account when you change phones.
  • Prices are shown in your local currency on the Google Play payment screen.
